# 📸 Instagram B2B Growth Engineering Skill

This skill defines the technical rules, retention frameworks, visual design standards, and conversion loops for engineering high-performing B2B Instagram content (Reels, Carousels, and Single-Stat Cards) for **TrackKaro AI**, **Vance AI SDR**, and tech founders.

---

## 🎨 1. The Obsidian Tech Design System

B2B buyers and tech founders on Instagram do not respond to generic lifestyle templates or bright Canva pastels. They engage with **high-status, dark-mode developer aesthetics**:

- **Canvas Dimensions:**
  - **Reels (Video):** `1080 x 1920` (9:16 Portrait, 30/60 fps)
  - **Carousels (Images):** `1080 x 1350` (4:5 Portrait — maximizes screen real estate)
- **Color Tokens:**
  - Obsidian Dark Background: `#06080d` / `#080a10`
  - Neon Accents: Emerald `#10b981` (Success / Fast), Rose `#f43f5e` (Warning / Decay), Cyan `#06b6d4` (Tech System), Amber `#f59e0b` (Alert)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Heavy weights: 800, 900)
  - Numbers, Metrics & Code: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 600, 800)
- **Glassmorphism:** `background: rgba(18, 22, 34, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(20px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08);`

## 📊 2. The 5-Slide Carousel Retention Framework

Carousels generate the highest save rate and algorithmic reach on Instagram when structured as a progressive narrative:

```
Slide 1: High-Stakes Contrarian Hook + Swipe Cue ("Swipe to see the math ➔")
Slide 2: The Brutal Mathematical Dropoff (Data Visualization / Benchmark)
Slide 3: The System Architecture (Node-by-Node Pipeline Blueprint)
Slide 4: Real Case Benchmark (Before vs After high-contrast comparison)
Slide 5: The Frictionless Call-to-Action ("Comment 'KEYWORD' for Free Audit")
```

### Critical Carousel Rules:
1. **Slide 1 must establish a clear enemy:** e.g., *"Why 78% of High-Ticket Ad Leads Ghost You in 5 Minutes"*, not *"How to improve sales response time"*.
2. **Slide 2 must validate the pain with numbers:** Show the mathematical curve (0-15s vs 5m vs 4h).
3. **Slide 3 must look like an engineering schematic:** Visual nodes with timestamps (`0.2s`, `8.4s`, `14.8s`).
4. **Slide 5 must give a binary, zero-risk offer:** Free 60s Speed Test, or Free 20 Custom ICP Pitches.

## 🎬 3. The 4-Scene Reel Retention Framework (10-14s Video)

Short-form videos that convert high-ticket B2B clients must follow a strict **non-overlapping 4-scene progression** using HyperFrames & GSAP:

```
Scene 1 (0.0s - 2.8s) [THE HOOK]     -> Big bold typography, glowing hazard badge, zero competing elements.
Scene 2 (2.8s - 5.8s) [THE PAIN]     -> Red neon card scales in: Manual sales rep taking 4h 12m (LEAD GHOSTED).
Scene 3 (5.8s - 8.8s) [THE SOLUTION] -> Emerald green bounce: TrackKaro/Vance 14.8s live counter + 4 feature badges.
Scene 4 (8.8s - 11.0s) [THE CTA]     -> Glowing domain button with pulsing gradient (trackkaroai.com / vance.trackkaroai.com).
```

### Reel Retention Rules:
- **Never stack all elements on screen at once:** Old scene exits before the new scene enters.
- **Micro-movements keep attention:** Subtle counter ticks, glowing borders, elastic eases (`back.out(1.4)`, `power4.out`).
- **Sound & Audio Cues:** Include subtle UI audio cues (chimes, clicks, sirens) on key transitions.

## 📲 5. The Autonomous Dispatch & 1-Tap Approval Loop

Content must be prepared automatically by AI and delivered to Telegram for manual human publishing:
1. Engine generates the media asset (MP4 Reel or 5 PNGs + PDF).
2. Engine generates the formatted caption with copyable code blocks.
3. Engine pushes alert to Telegram with `best_time_to_post` recommendation.
4. Founder reviews on mobile in 15 seconds, saves media to camera roll, copies caption, and posts natively from the Instagram app.
